The failure of fully parallel duplicate resolution reaffirms that placement validity requires context-sensitive, sequential refinement. Moving forward, research must pivot from offline heuristics toward minimal online constraint integration: either by enriching the crossover signature with mask-based feasibility information or by coupling GA with a fast differentiable post-processor that uses actual impedance gradients to fine-tune offspring. Adaptive weighting schemes and multi-objective balancing remain promising, but only if grounded in real electrical feedback rather than geometric proxies alone.
