Static heuristics with round-trip feasibility, 25th–30th percentile sparsification, and adaptive exponents consistently yield top performance. Simplicity and gentle penalties outperform complex look-ahead or centrality features. The 2-step residual cost estimator shows unexpected promise, suggesting lightweight path-awareness can enhance static matrices. Persistent challenges include balancing instance sizes and the static framework’s inability to adapt during tour construction. Future work should explore hybrid static-dynamic heuristics, refined sparsification thresholds, and learned feasibility predictors—prioritizing robustness over complexity.
