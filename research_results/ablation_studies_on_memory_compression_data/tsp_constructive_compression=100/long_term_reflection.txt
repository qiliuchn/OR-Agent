Constructive TSP heuristics peak with fixed, well-tuned MST-NN hybrid estimation and consistent two-step lookahead—adaptive or learned enhancements consistently underperform due to noise, fragility, or overhead. Simplicity, candidate pruning (top-10 NN), and balanced future-cost estimation (0.5/0.5 MST-NN) are key. Destination awareness and dynamic weighting add complexity without benefit. The stateless interface fundamentally limits global reasoning. Future gains require hybrid architectures: separate construction from lightweight local search (e.g., 2-opt), or explore efficient learned reranking of candidates using offline-trained models that respect computational constraints.
