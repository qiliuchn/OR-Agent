Stateless, statistical feature-based adaptive blending of Best Fit and Worst Fit remains the most effective approach, with explicit tight-fit and residual-space boosting providing consistent gains. Non-greedy mechanisms and stateful learning fail under online constraints or degrade performance. The ~2050-bin threshold (2.7% excess) marks a new plateau, suggesting fundamental limits of greedy, single-item heuristics. Future progress requires either lightweight within-instance state for distribution-aware residual targeting or hybrid architectures that combine statistical adaptation with minimal lookahead—all while preserving numerical stability and monotonicity.
