Static, well-tuned heuristics combining inverse distance (0.8), demand-distance ratio (0.3), depot return bonus (15.0), and capacity penalties (threshold 0.85, weight 120.0) remain dominant. Dynamic or complex mechanisms—clustering, residual capacity proxies, adaptive neighborhoods—consistently underperform due to noise or misalignment with POMO’s decoding dynamics. The only robust improvements come from subtle, instance-level adaptations like demand variability–based capacity thresholds. Future work must prioritize architecture-integrated, state-aware biasing that respects O(n²) efficiency while capturing true route progression, moving beyond static edge scoring toward dynamic, decoding-phase-conditioned guidance.
