Adaptive, scale-aware heuristics that modulate parameters by problem size (e.g., reducing depot bias for n>500) outperform fixed or complex learned heuristics, confirming that simplicity with strategic scaling is key. Static components remain essential, but discrete adaptation based on instance scale yields robust gains, especially on large instances. Persistent challenges include smooth parameter transitions and modeling sequential feasibility. Future work should explore continuous adaptation, better clustering signals, and hybrid geometric-decomposition approaches for scalability beyond 1000 nodes.
