Geometric heuristics derived from MDS-distorted coordinates degrade on larger CVRP instances, confirming that spatial proximity alone is insufficient without capacity-awareness. This reinforces prior findings: static edge biases cannot replace decoding-time adaptivity. Successful elements—entropy regularization, neural weight prediction, and demand-aware penalties—highlight the value of lightweight, instance-adaptive meta-features over complex geometric constructs. Future work must prioritize capacity-conditioned heuristics, robust non-Euclidean structural features, and dynamic biasing aligned with route construction progress, while maintaining vectorization and simplicity.
