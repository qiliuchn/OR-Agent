Lightweight, per-bin state memory—when robustly implemented—provides meaningful gains over purely stateless heuristics, confirming that minimal sequence-awareness enhances online decisions. However, state management complexity remains a critical barrier. The success of multi-level lookahead combined with adaptive distribution metrics (entropy, median) and carefully weighted memory components reveals a general principle: effective online bin packing balances immediate fit quality with anticipation of near-future patterns. Future work must prioritize stable, scalable state mechanisms—possibly externalized—and explore hybrid offline-online learning grounded in actual packing outcomes rather than heuristic proxies.
