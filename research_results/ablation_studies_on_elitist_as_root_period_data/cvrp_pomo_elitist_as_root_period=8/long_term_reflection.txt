Static, multi-component heuristics with instance-adaptive weighting remain optimal, but must balance complexity and stability. Multi-pass dynamic urgency simulation outperforms closed-form approximations, especially on large instances, confirming that route-progress sensitivity is crucial yet fragile. Geometric priors fail without true coordinates, while vectorized, capacity-aware components are essential. Future work should focus on lightweight, decoding-aware proxies grounded in high-quality data, problem-size-adaptive parameters, and robust fusion of the proven five-heuristic framework—avoiding over-engineering while preserving numerical stability.
