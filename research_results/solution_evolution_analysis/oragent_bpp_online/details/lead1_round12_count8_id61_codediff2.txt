    size modes. Then generates geometric progressions (e.g., φ^k, √2^k) centered on these emergent 
    modes rather than solely on the current item. This combines intra-instance memory concepts

    recent item distribution by analyzing the pattern of remaining bin capacities. Bins that have

    about the common sizes of items processed. We create an empirical distribution from the
    differences in bin capacities to infer recent item sizes and adjust our common-size sets
