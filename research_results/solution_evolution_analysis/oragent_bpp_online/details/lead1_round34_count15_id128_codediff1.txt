    size generation with high-performing static multiplier sets as a fallback.
    The strategy activates static multipliers when dynamic common size generation
    produces low-signal conditions (detected via coefficient of variation of
    dynamic_common_sizes or sparsity of unique remainders). This hybrid approach
    preserves the robustness of static strategies on adversarial instances like
    val_0/val_1 while retaining adaptability on diverse distributions, directly
    addressing the performance degradation observed in previous attempts.
    
    Implementation idea: Introduce high-performing static multiplier sets as a
    fallback baseline, but activate them only when dynamic common size generation
    fails to produce meaningful signals. Detect low-signal conditions via statistics
    like the coefficient of variation of dynamic_common_sizes or the sparsity of
    unique remainders, and switch to pre-tuned fractional multipliers in those cases.

        item: Size of the item to place
        bins_remain_cap: NumPy array of remaining bin capacities

        NumPy array of priority scores for each bin

    post_placement_caps = bins_remain_cap - item
