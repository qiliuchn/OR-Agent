ACO’s stochasticity amplifies low-level numerical and algorithmic effects into macroscopic performance variance.

This separation preserves the stability required for effective pheromone learning while enabling dynamic responsiveness to problem constraints.

- **Essential long jumps** to high-prize distant nodes are systematically suppressed by the squared-distance penalty, even when clearly feasible within the budget.
- **Static rigidity** prevents adaptation to clustered node layouts, skewed prize distributions, or varying distance scales.
- **Evaluation reliability** is compromised by ACO’s inherent stochasticity, necessitating ≥5 independent runs for trustworthy validation.
